# APL category guide — canonical knowledge base (2026-11-19)

Source: APL "Kategórialeírások AI-hoz" (Hungarian guide with examples). This is the canonical taxonomy
authority for APL data; the seven categories below replace previous naming where noted.

## Binding rules
- Exactly ONE category per post; when several styles co-occur, pick the one that dominates the post's
  primary message.
- **Holocaust denial takes absolute priority** over the dominance rule (legal background): open or coded
  denial, relativization, excusing perpetrators/collaborators -> Holocaust Denial, always.
- No mechanical keyword-only classification: keywords are aids only; full semantic and context analysis is
  mandatory, and surface keywords are often misleading.
- Any minimal doubt -> mark for review; APL experts correct it, and the correction feeds learning.

## Primary filter: IHRA working definition
Antisemitism is a certain perception of Jews, which may be expressed as hatred toward Jews; rhetorical and
physical manifestations target Jewish or non-Jewish individuals, their property, community institutions and
religious facilities. The IHRA 11 examples are binding references (killing calls, dehumanizing myths of
Jewish collective power, collective blame, denial, invented/exaggerated Holocaust accusations, dual-loyalty
claims, denial of Jewish self-determination, double standards, blood-libel symbols applied to Israel,
Nazi parallels, collective responsibility for Israel's actions).

## Anti-Israel antisemitism — the 3D test (Sharansky)
Criticism of Israel similar to criticism of any other country is NOT antisemitic. A statement is
antisemitic if ANY one holds: **Delegitimization** (denying the right to exist/self-determination, Israel as
a racist project), **Demonization** (Israel as absolute evil, Nazi/SS/Auschwitz equations, blood-libel
imagery), **Double standards** (demands made only of Israel). HU keywords: Israhell, genocídium, etnoállam,
terrorállam, cionnáci. HU narratives: the Peres-quote buy-out/colonization theory, migration-crisis blame,
dual-loyalty attacks.

## The seven categories
1. **Anti-Israel antisemitism** (Izrael-ellenes antiszemitizmus)
2. **Anti-Judaism** (Antijudaizmus) — attacks on the religion: Talmud demonization (fabricated quotes),
   God-killer charges, rabbi/synagogue attacks; HU: kóser vágás (sakter) framing, traditional dress mockery,
   Christian-national exclusivity claims.
3. **Conspiracy-theory-based antisemitism** (Összeesküvés-elmélet-alapú) — secret coordinated Jewish power:
   Rothschilds, world government, 9/11, COVID, blood libel; HU: Tiszaeszlár revival, Soros population-
   replacement narrative, zsidópóráz, judeobolsevizmus, kazárok.
4. **Classical antisemitism** (Klasszikus) — raw, direct hatred: slurs, dehumanization, violence calls;
   HU: Zsidesz, büdös/mocskos zsidók, zsidrák, Judapest; "zsidó" itself used as a slur.
5. **Latent antisemitism** (Látens) — RENAMED from Structural (change 2026-11-19). Coded, indirect:
   dogwhistle substitutes (globalists, international financial elite, background power, admins),
   echo coding (((name))), juice/watermelon emojis for Jews, money-bag/bank/rat/octopus motifs - and ONLY
   when context proves the coded meaning targets Jews (~90% of these terms in antisemitic discourse still
   need proof per case).
6. **Holocaust denial** (Holokauszttagadás) — denial, distortion, relativization, perpetrator excuses;
   HU: holokamu, holohoax, holománia, kamukauszt, holovallás; 6-million -> 271k myths; Szálasi/nyilas
   exoneration narratives.
7. **Not antisemitic** (Nem antiszemita) — educational/historical content, Jewish community's own posts,
   counterspeech and antisemitism-criticism even when quoting the hateful keywords.
